## Contradictions for review

1. **Shelf life:** store listing says `12 мес.`; the package says `Срок годности: 18 месяцев.`
2. **Storage temperature:** store listing says `при температуре не выше +25°C`; the package shows a storage range up to `+30°С`.
3. **Classification wording:** the store listing says `Минеральная, Питьевая`, while the legible package wording says `вода питьевая` and does not clearly show the word `минеральная`. This may be a catalog-classification difference; review before treating the product as mineral water.

The store description refers to deep wells in various regions of Russia, while the photographed label appears to contain a specific source/location block. That block is too small to read reliably, so no further contradiction is asserted.
